# Company logo sources

Each logo was downloaded from the company's own official website on 2026-10-06.
None was redrawn, traced, generated or reshaped. On the site they are shown as a
single-colour silhouette that suits the theme (see `CompanyStrip.module.css`);
the files themselves keep their original colours.

How InfoTech was identified: the CV names "InfoTech Private Limited, Lahore". InfoTech
(Private) Limited, trading as InfoTech Group (est. 1995, software and systems
integration), has its head office at 12-N Gulberg II, Lahore, and its official site is
infotechgroup.com. If this is not the right company, delete `infotech.png` and the
strip falls back to the name as text.
