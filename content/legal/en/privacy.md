To keep the contact form from being misused for spam, the contact page uses hCaptcha, a service of Intuition Machines, Inc. (USA). hCaptcha checks whether the input comes from a human. For this, data such as the IP address, browser and device information and the behaviour during the check are sent to hCaptcha when the contact page loads and when the check is solved; hCaptcha may set its own cookies or similar storage. <!-- form:web3forms -->The result of the check is sent to Web3Forms with the form.<!-- /form --><!-- form:server -->The website's server asks hCaptcha whether the result is genuine before a message is sent.<!-- /form --> hCaptcha is not loaded on any other page of this website.

The legal basis is the legitimate interest in protecting the form and my inbox from automated misuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More information: hCaptcha's privacy policy at [hcaptcha.com/privacy](https://www.hcaptcha.com/privacy).

## Audience measurement with Vercel Web Analytics

The published website uses Vercel Web Analytics. It counts page views without cookies and, for this, records among other things the page visited, the referring website, the approximate country, and the device type, operating system and browser. According to Vercel, visitors are not tracked across websites; for counting, a short-lived, irreversible value is derived from the request and discarded after about 24 hours.

The legal basis is the legitimate interest in understanding the use of the website in aggregated form (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## The site's own visit statistics

In addition, this website counts its visits itself. Only aggregated numbers are stored, in a database (Upstash Redis, connected through Vercel):

- per day: visits and page views,
- per day and country: visits and page views per two-letter country code,
- running totals: visits, page views and per country,
- page views by hour and weekday (German time),
- per day: views per page of the website,
- per day: page views per language of the website (for example "de"),
- per day: device type (phone, tablet, computer),
- per day: the referring website, as a domain only (for example "linkedin.com"), never the full address,
- per day: the colour theme used (light or dark),
- per day: CV downloads per CV language, and clicks on LinkedIn, GitHub and "Ask me a question",
- the date counting started and the time of the most recent page view,
- the last 25 page views, each with country, page and time only.

These numbers are kept **without a time limit**, so that the statistics cover every day since counting began.

Deliberately not stored or logged:

- IP addresses, browser identifiers (user agent) or any other identifiers,
- cities, regions or coordinates (only the country),
- cookies; the counting does not use any browser storage either,
- fingerprinting or tracking across websites.

Visits are not counted when the browser sends "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control", nor are requests from search engines and other automated programs. Counting happens only on the published website, not in preview versions.

The legal basis is the legitimate interest in measuring the reach of the website in aggregated, non-personal form (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Cookies and browser storage

This website sets exactly one cookie of its own, and only when you choose a language in the language menu: "NEXT_LOCALE" contains nothing but the language code (for example "de"), so that you see the website in that language again on your next visit. It is valid for one year and is not used for statistics or any other purpose. For hCaptcha on the contact page, see above.

Apart from that, only these settings are stored in the browser:

- the chosen colour theme (light or dark), in local storage (localStorage),
- whether the short opening animation has already been shown in this session (sessionStorage),
- on the owner's device only: a note that the owner's own visits are not counted (localStorage).

The cookie and these entries are necessary for the functions you use (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG) and contain no identifier that could recognise you.
