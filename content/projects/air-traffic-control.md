An original top-down 2D game (Air Traffic Control is the working title), developed under the name MHC Studio. You draw flight paths with your finger to land aircraft safely and keep them from colliding.

## Status

- **Built with** Unity and C#.
- **Platforms:** iPad and iPhone (iOS) and Android.
- **In development.** The game runs on iPad; testing on Android comes later. It is not in any app store yet.

The code, the game plan and clickable screen mockups are in the repository.
